scoreboard cards) instead of real photos, since I don't have access to your
actual match photos.

Drop your real images in this folder with these names and they'll slot
straight into the site (you'll just need to add a couple of <img> tags
where noted in the HTML — ask me and I'll wire them in):

  logo.svg              -> already here (bat + ball mark). Replace with your
                            real logo if you have one designed.
  hero-action-shot.jpg   -> a good action photo for the homepage hero
                            (bowler/batsman mid-shot, like your old site had)
  venue-dropouts.jpg     -> a real photo of Dropouts Turf, Vasai West
  venue-alexarena.jpg    -> photo of Alex Arena (Season 2 venue)
  season1-winners.jpg    -> team photo of Season 1 champions
  season2-winners.jpg    -> team photo of Season 2 champions
  gallery-01.jpg ... 06  -> match day photos for a future gallery section

Recommended sizes: hero shot ~1200x1400px, venue/winner shots ~1200x800px,
all compressed to keep the site fast (use tinypng.com or similar before
uploading).
